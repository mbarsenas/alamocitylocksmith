Commercial locksmith FAQs

Can you rekey an entire office?

Yes. The scope depends on the number and compatibility of the locks. Call with the door count and access requirements for planning.

What is a master-key system?

It is a coordinated lock system in which individual keys open selected doors while a master key can open a broader authorized group.
