Residential locksmith FAQs

Should I rekey or replace my locks?

Rekeying is often appropriate when the lock hardware is in good condition and you only need old keys to stop working. Damaged or outdated hardware may need replacement.

Can you make all my doors use one key?

Often yes, when the lock cylinders are compatible. A technician can inspect the hardware and explain the available options.
